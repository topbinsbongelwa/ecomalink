How it works

Three steps, one wallet, no branch visit.

Ecomalink runs on your phone. Top up once, then send to any linked wallet, bank account, or cash-out agent in a corridor country.

01 / Fund

Load your wallet

Top up from a bank card, an agent, or a linked mobile money account. Funds are available in your Ecomalink balance immediately.

02 / Send

Pick a recipient

Choose a country, enter a phone number or account, and confirm the amount. Ecomalink shows the exact exchange rate and fee before you send.

03 / Arrive

Money lands fast

Your recipient gets a notification and can cash out at an agent, move it to their bank, or keep it in their own Ecomalink wallet.
